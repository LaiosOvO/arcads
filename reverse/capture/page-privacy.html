Additional information regarding Shopify integration

If you connect a Shopify store to the Platform, we access the store through Shopify's API with read-only permissions. We process the store's catalogue (product titles, descriptions, prices and images), its order and sales figures, and its customer records: first and last name, email address, phone number, country, number of orders, total amount spent, customer tags, and account creation and update dates. We also store the store's domain and name, the permissions granted, and encrypted authentication tokens.

Roles. For the personal data of the store's customers, the merchant who connects the store is the data controller and FRESHR acts as its processor, processing that data only on the merchant's instructions and to provide the features the merchant uses. For the merchant's own account and connection data, FRESHR remains the data controller as described in section 1, and the legal basis is the performance of our contract with the merchant.

We process this data for the following purposes: to authenticate and maintain the Shopify connection; to import the brand's products and prices into the Platform; to show the merchant its customer base and sales figures; to identify, at the merchant's request, which of its customers are social media creators; and to secure, troubleshoot and monitor the integration.

Creator matching. When the merchant asks the Platform to find creators among its customers, we send each customer's email address to our creator data provider, influencers.club, which acts as our sub-processor and returns the public social media profiles associated with that address, if any. We then retrieve publicly available information about those profiles, such as the handle, follower count and engagement rate. This lookup runs only when the merchant starts it.

Sharing and storage. Shopify data is stored on our hosting infrastructure (AWS in the European Union) and may be processed by our service providers solely to provide the features the merchant requests, including artificial intelligence providers when the merchant asks the Platform's assistant about its store or its customers. We do not sell Shopify data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Retention and deletion. Store and customer data are kept for as long as the store is connected to the Platform. When the merchant disconnects the store from its last brand or uninstalls the Arcads app from Shopify, we delete the encrypted authentication tokens and the store's customer records. When Shopify forwards a customer or shop data erasure request, we delete the corresponding records. Customers of a Shopify store who wish to exercise their rights regarding their personal data should contact the merchant, who remains their point of contact; we assist the merchant in responding to these requests.

Additional information regarding TikTok integration

If you choose to connect your TikTok account to the Platform, we process the personal data strictly necessary to provide this integration, including your TikTok account identifier (open_id), username, display name and profile picture, connection status, the permissions you granted, encrypted authentication tokens, and metadata about the videos published on your TikTok account (video identifier, title, description, duration, cover image, link to the post, publication date, and view, like, comment and share counts). If you allow the Platform to post to TikTok, we also process the videos, captions and post settings you choose to publish, and information about each publication (its status, the TikTok publication identifier and, for public posts, the post identifier and link).

We process this data for the following purposes:

  • to authenticate and maintain your TikTok connection;
  • to display the connected TikTok account and its recent videos inside the Platform;
  • if you allow posting, to publish to your TikTok account the videos you approve, with the settings you choose, and to show you the status of each publication;
  • to secure, troubleshoot and monitor the integration.

OAuth scopes. To provide this integration, we request limited TikTok permissions: user.info.basic, which lets us read your account identifier, display name and profile picture so we can show you which account is connected, and video.list, which lets us read the list of videos published on that account and their public metrics. Where posting is available to you, we also request video.publish, which lets us publish to your TikTok account a video you have approved. You can decline that permission on TikTok's consent screen and still use the read-only features.

Publishing to TikTok. We publish a video only after you have reviewed it and clicked "Post" in the Platform's TikTok post form. The Platform's assistant may suggest a video and a caption, but it cannot publish on its own and cannot choose your post settings. In the form, you see the TikTok account the post goes to, a preview of the video and the caption, and you choose who can view the video, whether others can comment, duet or stitch it, whether it promotes your own brand or a third party (TikTok's commercial content disclosure), and whether TikTok should label it as AI-generated. Only videos created or uploaded in the Platform can be published; videos imported from other platforms cannot. When you click "Post", we send TikTok the video file, the caption and these settings. We keep a record of the post you approved (the caption, the selected video, the settings, the TikTok account it was sent to, the time of approval and the user who approved it) so that we can show you its status and evidence your approval. TikTok may take a few minutes to process the video before it appears on your profile, and TikTok may apply its own review and restrictions to it.

What we do not do. We do not publish anything to your TikTok account without your approval of that specific post. We do not edit or delete content already on your TikTok account. We do not read your private messages, your followers or your audience data. We do not sell TikTok data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence models.

Sharing and storage. The connected TikTok account and the data we read through it are visible to the members of your workspace, who can also prepare and approve posts to it. Your TikTok profile picture and the cover images of your videos are copied to our hosting infrastructure (including AWS in the European Union) so that they can be displayed inside the Platform. Authentication tokens are stored encrypted. The list of your videos and their metrics is read from TikTok each time you open the corresponding screen. The videos you publish to TikTok are sent from the copy already stored in your workspace; publishing does not create an additional copy. Your TikTok username, the captions you publish and the status of your posts may be processed by our artificial intelligence service providers solely so that the Platform's assistant can prepare posts with you and report their status.

The legal basis for this processing is the performance of a contract and/or the implementation of pre-contractual measures taken at your request when you choose to use the TikTok integration. Where strictly necessary to secure and monitor the integration and to keep evidence of your approval of a post, the legal basis is our legitimate interest.

Data related to the TikTok integration are kept for as long as necessary to provide the feature. Connection data, encrypted tokens and integration status are retained until you disconnect the integration, your account is deleted, or the integration is discontinued. Records of the posts you approved are kept for 12 months after the post was sent to TikTok; drafts that were not published are deleted 30 days after they were cancelled, expired or failed. When you disconnect the integration, we ask TikTok to revoke our access and immediately delete your authentication tokens. We keep your TikTok account identifier, username and display name, together with the records of the posts made through it, for up to 12 months after disconnection, then delete them. Copies of your public profile picture and video cover images may remain in our image storage after you disconnect. You can also withdraw our access at any time from your TikTok account settings. Disconnecting does not remove videos already published to TikTok; you can delete them from TikTok directly.

When you use the TikTok integration, TikTok processes the content you publish and the information it receives through the integration as an independent controller, in accordance with TikTok's applicable terms and privacy policy.

Permissions. Viewing your ads. Your ads and their metrics are read from TikTok each time you open "My ads", and kept in a short-lived cache for up to two minutes. We do not store them in our database. Previews are loaded directly from TikTok.

Importing ads. When you add a TikTok ad to the chat, we copy its video into your workspace's ads library, and our artificial intelligence service providers (including Google Cloud Vertex AI and Anthropic) analyse it on our behalf and under our instructions. Uploading videos to TikTok. We upload a video to your TikTok advertiser account only after you have approved that specific upload in the Platform. The Platform's assistant may suggest the upload, but it cannot upload without your approval. The video is added to your advertiser account's library; we do not create, edit, publish or delete ads, and you decide in TikTok Ads Manager whether to use it in an ad. We keep a record of each upload you approved (the video, the advertiser account, the user who approved it and its status).

What we do not do. We do not create, edit, pause or delete campaigns, ad groups or ads. We do not change your budgets or your billing. We do not use your TikTok ads or metrics for other customers, sell them, use them for advertising, or use them to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence models.

Sharing and storage. The connected TikTok for Business account and the data we read through it are visible to the members of your workspace. Imported ads are stored on our hosting infrastructure (including AWS in the European Union). Authentication tokens are stored encrypted. Videos you upload are sent to TikTok from the copy already stored in your workspace.

The legal basis for this processing is the performance of a contract and/or the implementation of pre-contractual measures taken at your request when you choose to use the TikTok for Business integration. Where strictly necessary to secure and monitor the integration and to keep evidence of your approval of an upload, the legal basis is our legitimate interest.

Connection data and encrypted tokens are retained until you disconnect the integration, your account is deleted, or the integration is discontinued. When you disconnect, we immediately delete your authentication token, stop reading data from TikTok, remove the advertiser accounts linked to your brands, and ask TikTok to revoke our access unless another workspace still uses the same authorisation. Imported ads remain in your ads library until you delete them or your workspace is deleted. If TikTok ends our access to its API, we delete all ads imported from TikTok. Records of the uploads you approved are kept while your workspace exists. Disconnecting does not remove videos already uploaded to TikTok; you can delete them from TikTok Ads Manager directly.

When you use the TikTok for Business integration, TikTok processes the information it receives through the integration as an independent controller, in accordance with TikTok's applicable terms and privacy policy.
